02 IDEAFORGE 2K26 / OFFICIAL PARTICIPANT GUIDELINES

Hackathon
rules & regulations.

6-Hour Hackathon • Open Innovation • Hardware or Software • AI Tools Permitted

Read the guidelines before registering and keep them in mind throughout the event.

01

Event format & theme

The hackathon runs for 6 hours. The official theme will be announced 2 days before the event. Teams may build software, hardware, or hybrid solutions, and every project must address the announced theme.

02

Team & registration

Each team may have a maximum of 5 members. Only registered members may participate. Teams must check in on time and follow organiser instructions.

03

Prototype & submission

Submit a working prototype before the deadline; a concept, presentation, mock-up, or video alone is not enough. Be ready to demonstrate core features and explain the problem, solution, and technology. Stop development when time is called. Late submissions may be penalised or disqualified.

04

AI tools & originality

AI tools, libraries, frameworks, APIs, and datasets are allowed. Test and understand your work, and disclose major AI tools or third-party components if asked. Plagiarism, copying another team’s project, or presenting existing work as original is prohibited.

05

Safety & conduct

Use only data, devices, accounts, and networks you are authorised to access. Respect privacy; do not build harmful or illegal functionality. Operate hardware safely and respect other teams, judges, volunteers, organisers, equipment, and venue property.

06

Judging & decisions

Judges may evaluate innovation, theme relevance, functionality, technical execution, feasibility, impact, and demonstration. Explain each member’s contribution honestly. Judges’ decisions and reasonable organiser instructions are final.

PARTICIPANT CHECKLIST

Check in on time • Build a working prototype within 6 hours • Submit before the deadline • Prepare a live demo • Follow safety and organiser instructions
